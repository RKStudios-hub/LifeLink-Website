Questions, answered

Short answers about how LifeLink works.

What is LifeLink?

LifeLink is a Community Emergency Service System. It helps you find nearby hospitals, check important details, see a route, and take the next step, all in one mobile-friendly app.

Does LifeLink work without internet?

The current prototype works offline so its main features can be used without a connection. The full version will go online for real-time updates, live maps, location tracking and road routing.

Is it free to download?

Yes. LifeLink is free to download and use.

How do I install it?

Tap Download above. On Android, install the APK. On any phone, you can also open the web app and choose "Add to Home Screen" to install it as a PWA.

Does it track my location?

No constant tracking. LifeLink asks for your location only when you need it, for example to find nearby hospitals and show a route.

Can I rely on it for medical decisions?

No. LifeLink does not replace doctors, hospitals, ambulances or professional medical judgement. The prototype may use sample or available data, so always confirm details with the hospital. In a life-threatening emergency, call 108.

What technology does it use?

HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Leaflet and OpenStreetMap for maps, OSRM for routes and distance, and PWA with a Service Worker for offline use.

What's coming next?

Real-time ambulance availability, verified blood donors by group and area, hospital ICU updates, verified volunteers, local emergency alerts, voice search, and Hindi and other regional languages.
